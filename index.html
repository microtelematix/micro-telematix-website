Who We Are

About Micro Telematix

Micro Telematix, a Government of India registered enterprise, is an Indian OEM specializing in innovative electronic products for water conservation, renewable energy, security and automation applications.

For over three decades, we have delivered reliable, eco-friendly and cost-effective solutions for residential, commercial, industrial, educational, healthcare and government institutions.

Our products are designed to save water, conserve energy, improve operational efficiency and provide peace of mind through automation and intelligent control systems.

Our mission is to provide reliable, environmentally responsible technologies that help customers save resources, reduce costs and improve operational efficiency.

Founder

Micro Telematix was founded by Jitendra R. Kapoor, an accomplished Electronics Engineer with a B.Tech. (Honours) in Electronics & Communication Engineering from IIT Kharagpur and an M.Tech. in Information Technology.

With extensive experience in research, product design and systems engineering, including professional experience with ISRO and projects associated with NASA, he established Micro Telematix with a commitment to practical innovation, engineering excellence and dependable product design.

His technical expertise continues to guide the company's focus on quality, reliability and environmentally responsible technologies that deliver practical solutions for homes, businesses and industry.
